### Core Feature Requirements:

1. Advanced Quick-Access SOS Hub:
   - High-contrast, floating emergency action bar with one-touch triggers:
     * Police (112), Ambulance (108), Fire (101), Women Helpline (1091), Senior Citizen Support (14567).
   - "Broadcast SOS" Feature: Copies real-time GPS coordinates and opens a pre-filled emergency SMS/WhatsApp template for trusted contacts.
   - Silent / Camouflage SOS: Discreet alert trigger for personal safety.
   - Medical Emergency ID: Fast modal displaying blood group, emergency contacts, and vital medical notes.
   - Deterrent Tool: Built-in loud siren / screen strobe toggle for emergency situations.

2. "Nagpur Today" (Live Civic Impact & Transparency Counter):
   - A modern, scannable real-time impact metrics bar with distinct status icons and pulsing "Live NMC Feed" indicator.

3. Swachh Nagpur & Waste Grievance Management (Grievance-to-NMC Dispatch):
   - Quick photo-upload grievance form with auto-geolocation tag.
   - Ticket Tracker Card: Shows real-time progress of submitted issues (Registered ➔ Assigned to Sanitation Van / Official ➔ Resolved).
   - Instant dynamic increment: Submitting a report adds an active ticket and updates the "Nagpur Today" live statistics.

4. Healthcare & Transit Hub:
   - Filterable directory for nearby 24/7 pharmacies, blood units availability, and emergency hospitals.
   - Nagpur Metro schedule and feeder transit finder.

5. Viksit Nagpur Open Innovation:
   - "Nagpur 2047" community suggestion board with upvotes and status badges.

6. Accessibility & Multilingual Support:
   - Toggle support for English, मराठी, and हिंदी.
   - High-contrast emergency buttons with clear visual icons.
